Key Architecture

Designed for Discerning Readers

Traditional filters break or mute entire accounts. Hide The Annoying uses semantic intelligence to preserve what matters.

🤖

True Semantic AI, Not Dumb Keyword Lists

Keyword mutes are notoriously blunt: blocking "sale" hides your favorite indie game release, while blocking "coin" hides historical artifacts. Powered by TypeSafe Jev (System One), Hide The Annoying evaluates context in ~70–300ms, distinguishing between a Steam discount discussion and predatory crypto shilling.

🎛️

Soft Collapse Philosophy

Complete timeline removals cause jarring layout shifts and disorienting gaps. Our 40px summary bar lets you know something was filtered, why it was flagged, and lets you view it with 1 click.

🔒

Zero Tracking & Privacy

Your preferences, cached decisions, and API keys remain strictly inside your browser's chrome.storage.local. No analytics scripts, no tracking pixels, and no data harvesting.

⚡

High-Speed Local Cache

Never wait twice. Evaluated tweets and handles are instantly cached in memory and indexed in local storage, guaranteeing 60 FPS smooth scrolling down endless feeds.

📋

Allowlist & Blocklist Control

Love following a specific political journalist or sports analyst? Add them to your Allowlist with a single click. Their posts will stay pristine and uncollapsed forever.

⭐ 100% Open Source

Auditable, Transparent, and Community-Driven

Every line of code is open source on GitHub. Inspect how decision models classify tweets, verify that zero user data is ever transmitted, or contribute your own features and improvements.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Hide The Annoying open source and free to use? +
Yes! Hide The Annoying is 100% free and open source. You can inspect the entire codebase, audit how decision models classify posts, verify our zero-telemetry privacy architecture, or contribute on GitHub at github.com/turkerdev/hide-the-annoying.
What is TypeSafe Jev System One? +
TypeSafe Jev is a specialized, calibrated decision model designed for ultra-low latency classification (~70–300ms). Unlike massive 70B parameter chat models, System One delivers swift, deterministic, structured choices ideal for real-time web extension workloads.
Does this extension track my personal browsing or direct messages? +
No. The extension only evaluates publicly visible tweet text on x.com and twitter.com timelines. It does not access private messages, cookies, passwords, or personal history. Please review our Privacy Policy for full disclosures.
Can I customize which topics get collapsed? +
Yes! In the extension popup or options dashboard, you can individually toggle Politics & Ragebait, Soccer/Football, or Finance & Crypto. You can also define custom keywords and block specific topics according to your own taste.
What happens when I click "Always Allow"? +
Clicking "Always Allow" adds that account handle to your personal Allowlist. The extension immediately unhides their tweets across the page and will never filter them again, regardless of what topics they discuss.
Which browsers are supported? +
Hide The Annoying is built according to standard Manifest V3 specifications and works seamlessly on Google Chrome, Brave, Arc, Microsoft Edge, Opera, and any other Chromium-based browser.
