Questions

Is it free?

The extension is free, and the source is on GitHub. Gmail sign-in includes a daily quota. A TypeSafe key skips that limit and calls Jev directly.

What is Jev?

TypeSafe Jev is the classifier. A post comes back in about 70–300ms. It is built for a yes-or-no decision, not a long reply.

Does it read my messages?

It reads public posts on x.com and twitter.com: the text, the name, and the handle. It does not read direct messages, cookies, or passwords. The privacy page has the rest.

Can I choose the topics?

Yes. Politics, soccer, and crypto can each be turned off. You can add your own topic. Always Allow keeps an account. A blocklist removes one.

What does Always Allow do?

That account stays visible, including posts about politics, soccer, or crypto.

Which browsers?

Chrome, Brave, Arc, Edge, Opera, and other Chromium browsers.

A classification takes about 70–300ms. Nothing in the extension is telemetry.
